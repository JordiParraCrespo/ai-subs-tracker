A value a vendor or a login file got wrong no longer fails the sync for every account on the machine: an empty window key, a reset outside years 1970–9999, a window of 1e308 minutes, a transcript dated in year 275760 and an over-long email are fitted to the contract where a reading is built. The API takes a sync as large as its contract allows (the JSON limit was 100 KB) and answers a larger body with 413 instead of 500.

`@flama/usage-providers/testing` is new: `FakeMachine`, `FakeVendors` and the vendors' bodies, shared by the unit specs, the desktop collector's soak and the e2e desktop suite.
