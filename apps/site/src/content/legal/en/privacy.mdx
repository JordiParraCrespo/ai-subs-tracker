## This website

This site sets no cookies, loads no analytics and calls no third-party service. The server that hosts it may keep standard access logs (IP address, time, the page requested) to keep it running.

## The desktop app

**Stays on your computer:** your Claude Code and Codex credentials, including refresh tokens; your prompts, code and transcripts; your shell and Claude settings, which the app changes only when you opt in.

**Reaches the Subtrack API:** for each account, its email, plan name, limit percentages and reset times, and token counts per model and day.

Provider credentials are sent only to their own provider, and Subtrack never refreshes them.

## Your choices

You can remove an account from the app at any time, and undo the opt-in shell and status line changes from its settings.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the project's repository.
