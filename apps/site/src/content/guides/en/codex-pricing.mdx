## Pro is now three tiers

On 29 September 2026 OpenAI split ChatGPT Pro into **$100, $200 and $500** tiers. All three meter only a weekly limit, with no 5-hour window.

<Callout>
  Codex reports every Pro tier with the same name, so ResetOwl cannot tell them apart. It assumes $200 until you set the price you actually pay, which then feeds every figure it shows.
</Callout>

## Limits on Go, Plus and Business

Go, Plus and Business meter both a **5-hour** window and a **weekly** one. When either runs out, Codex stops until it resets, which is where a second account helps. The [usage limits guide](/claude-code-usage-limits/) covers both vendors' windows, and [Claude Code pricing](/claude-code-pricing/) compares Anthropic's plans.
