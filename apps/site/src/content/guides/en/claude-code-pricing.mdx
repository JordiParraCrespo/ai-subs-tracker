## Which limits each plan meters

Most plans meter two windows: a **5-hour session window** and a **weekly** one. Ultra only meters the weekly window. The [usage limits guide](/claude-code-usage-limits/) explains how the two interact and what happens when you hit one.

## How to choose a plan

- **Pro** suits steady, lighter use. Watch the 5-hour window: it is the one you hit first.
- **Max** gives five times Pro's usage for five times the price, which matters when you regularly run out mid-session.
- **Ultra** drops the 5-hour window entirely, so long sessions only count against the week.

Comparing them side by side is the [Pro vs Max vs Ultra guide](/claude-pro-vs-max-vs-ultra/). If you also use OpenAI's agent, see [Codex pricing](/codex-pricing/).
